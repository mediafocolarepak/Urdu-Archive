## 1. Credits and tokens

The archive grows through two kinds of contribution, and each has its own sign.

| | **Credits** | **Tokens** |
|---|---|---|
| For | Work done as a task: translation, typing, proofreading | A gift: a text proposed, a voice lent to a text |
| Earned by | Operators, through tasks | Anyone signed in, outside tasks |
| Value in money | Yes, through compensation runs (REWARD_POLICY.md §5) | **None. Tokens are never converted, bought, sold or transferred (Owner)** |
| Measured | A rate per page, per category | One token per accepted contribution |
| Policy | REWARD_POLICY.md | This document |

**Why no money.** A gift that is paid stops being a gift: people start to count, and the archive starts to
receive more small or repeated contributions. Whoever wants a compensation already has the way to it: becoming
an Operator and taking tasks.

**The name (Owner).** "Token of appreciation", or simply "token", in English for everyone and in every language
of the app, as "credits" is.

## 2. What earns a token

A token is given for a contribution that has been **accepted**, never for simply sending something.

| Contribution | When the token is given | Counted for the past |
|---|---|---|
| A proposal: text, PDF, audio, video | When the proposal is published | Yes, from the records of Proposals (migration 112) |
| A reading aloud | When the reading is approved | Yes, from the records of Read aloud (migrations 113, 114) |
| A problem report that leads to a correction | When whoever fixes it marks "this report led to a correction" | No: today nothing records it |
| Reviewing Urdu titles | When Reward receives a reviewer's answers | No: the review has not started |

Not counted, because they already have their own sign: work done as a task (credits), Formation Paths (certificates),
posts on the Boards.

_To decide — Reward:_
- One token per contribution of any kind, as proposed, or a different measure for very long work (a whole book)?
  The proposal is one token each, so as not to put a price on gratitude.
- For reviewing Urdu titles: one token per batch of answers, or per number of titles? Which number?
- Should other contributions count (for example a Formation module written by a member)?

## 3. Who receives tokens

- Anyone signed in: Users and Operators. An Operator receives tokens only for what they give outside their tasks.
- Whoever approves a contribution never approves their own (GOVERNANCE.md principle 2): the token goes to the
  person who proposed or read, never to whoever approved.

## 4. How tokens are recorded

- **Automatically**, when the contribution is accepted. Nobody writes tokens by hand.
- **In a ledger that is only added to**, like the credits ledger: every token says who received it, for what record,
  and when.
- **Reward can annul a token**, with a written note (for example a proposal later found to be a copy). The annulment
  is a new line in the ledger: nothing is deleted.

## 5. What each person sees

- In **My Space**: how many tokens they have, and the list of what they gave.
- **What their gift has done**, which is the most meaningful thanks: for example how many times their reading has been
  listened to, or their text opened. _To decide — Reward:_ whether to count this. Today the app does not count
  listens or openings, and counting them must not record who listened.

## 6. Milestones and the certificate

- At certain milestones a person receives a **certificate of appreciation**, signed by the Reward lead, like the
  certificates of the Formation Paths (same mechanism, with a code anyone can verify).
- _To decide — Reward:_ the milestones (how many tokens), and the text of the certificate.

## 7. Public thanks (Owner)

- **A monthly thank-you** to those who gave, for example in Hayat or in the community: prepared by Reward,
  published by Communication.
- **Only for those who agree**, with a consent they can withdraw at any time, as for the reader's name on a reading.
- **No rankings and no comparisons**: the thanks names people and what they gave, not who gave more.
- _To decide — Reward, with HR:_ how to treat people under 18 (the app knows dates of birth).

## 8. Contributions made before tokens existed (Owner)

- They count. When tokens start, every proposal already published and every reading already approved gives its
  token once, marked "before tokens existed".
- Contributions from before the Proposals door (bulk imports, the original archive) don't record who gave what,
  so they cannot be counted person by person.

## 9. Controls

- Reward looks at a monthly summary: tokens given, by kind, and any annulments.
- Signs to watch: one contribution split into many small proposals, the same text proposed again, a sudden run of
  proposals from one person. With tokens worth no money the risk is low, but it is watched.
- Misuse is handled with HR, as for the Boards: Reward proposes, the HR lead decides, never one person alone.

## 10. What will be built, once this is approved

- The token ledger, and the automatic token when a proposal is published or a reading approved.
- The one-time count of past contributions.
- The "this report led to a correction" mark, for those who fix problems.
- A section in My Space, the certificate, and a view for Reward with the monthly summary.
- The consent for public thanks.
