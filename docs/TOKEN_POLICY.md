## 1. Credits and tokens

The archive grows through two kinds of contribution, and each has its own sign.

| | **Credits** | **Tokens** |
|---|---|---|
| For | Work done as a task: translation, typing, proofreading | A gift: a text proposed, a voice lent to a text |
| Earned by | Operators, through tasks | Anyone signed in, outside tasks |
| Value in money | Yes, through compensation runs (REWARD_POLICY.md §5) | **None. Tokens are never converted, bought, sold or transferred** |
| Measured | A rate per page, per category | By the size of the work: pages or minutes |
| Numbers set in | The Reward department's page | The Reward department's page |
| Policy | REWARD_POLICY.md | This document |

**Why no money.** A gift that is paid stops being a gift: people start to count, and the archive starts to
receive more small or repeated contributions. Whoever wants a compensation already has the way to it: becoming
an Operator and taking tasks.

**The name.** "Token of appreciation", or simply "token", in English for everyone and in every language of the app,
as "credits" is.

## 2. What earns tokens

Tokens are given for a contribution that has been **accepted**, never for simply sending something. Each accepted
contribution gives tokens once, in a number that recognizes its size.

| Contribution | When | How many | Counted for the past |
|---|---|---|---|
| A proposal of a text or PDF | When the proposal is published | By its pages | Yes, from the records of Proposals (migration 112) |
| A proposal of audio or video | When the proposal is published | By its minutes | Yes, as above |
| A reading aloud | When the reading is approved | By its minutes | Yes, from the records of Read aloud (migrations 113, 114) |
| A problem report that leads to a correction | When whoever fixes it marks "this report led to a correction" | One unit | No: today nothing records it |
| Reviewing Urdu titles | When Reward receives a reviewer's answers | One unit for each batch of answers sent | No: the review has not started |

- **How many tokens a page or a minute is worth** is set in the Reward department's page, like the credit rates,
  and changed in the same way (proposed by one person, approved by another).
- **The size comes from the record:** the pages of a text, the length of a recording. Whoever approves a
  contribution checks that it is filled in. Where a past contribution has no size recorded, it counts as one unit,
  and Reward may correct it.
- **Not counted**, because they already have their own sign: work done as a task (credits), Formation Paths
  (certificates), posts on the Boards. Other kinds of contribution may be added later, by a change to this document.

## 3. Who receives tokens

- Anyone signed in: Users and Operators. An Operator receives tokens only for what they give outside their tasks.
- Whoever approves a contribution never approves their own (GOVERNANCE.md principle 2): the tokens go to the
  person who proposed or read, never to whoever approved.

## 4. How tokens are recorded

- **Automatically**, when the contribution is accepted. Nobody writes tokens by hand.
- **In a ledger that is only added to**, like the credits ledger: every entry says who received the tokens, how many,
  for what record, and when.
- **Only Reward annuls tokens**, with a written note (for example a proposal later found to be a copy of a text
  already in the archive). Whoever approved the contribution and notices the problem tells Reward. The annulment is a
  new entry in the ledger: nothing is deleted.

## 5. What each person sees

- In **My Space**: how many tokens they have, and the list of what they gave.
- **What their gift has done**: how many times each reading of theirs has been listened to, and each text of theirs
  opened. The app counts this anonymously: the count never records who listened or opened.

## 6. Milestones and the certificate

- At each milestone a person receives a **certificate of appreciation**, like the certificates of the Formation
  Paths (same mechanism, with a code anyone can verify).
- **There are several milestones, rising.** How many tokens each one needs is set in the Reward department's page,
  like the credit rates.
- **The certificate is signed by a Focolare responsible.** Its sentence, in English, is written in the Reward
  department's page.

## 7. Public thanks

- **Every month, in the app and in the magazine (Hayat):** a thank-you to those who gave, prepared by Reward and
  published by Communication.
- **Only for those who agree**, with a consent they can withdraw at any time, as for the reader's name on a reading.
- **No rankings and no comparisons**: the thanks names people and what they gave, not who gave more.
- **People under 18 are never named in public.** They are thanked privately, in My Space, with a special word of
  encouragement. The app knows their age from the date of birth.

## 8. Contributions made before tokens existed

- They count. When tokens start, every proposal already published and every reading already approved gives its
  tokens once, marked "before tokens existed".
- Contributions from before the Proposals door (bulk imports, the original archive) don't record who gave what,
  so they cannot be counted person by person.

## 9. Controls

- Reward looks at a monthly summary: tokens given, by kind, and any annulments.
- Signs to watch: one contribution split into many small proposals, the same text proposed again, a sudden run of
  proposals from one person. With tokens worth no money the risk is low, but it is watched.
- Misuse is handled with HR, as for the Boards: Reward proposes, the HR lead decides, never one person alone.

## 10. What will be built, once this is approved

- The token ledger, and the tokens given automatically when a proposal is published or a reading approved,
  by pages or minutes.
- In the Reward department's page: the value of a page and of a minute, the milestones, the certificate's sentence
  and the word of encouragement for those under 18, each changed by proposal and approval.
- The one-time count of past contributions.
- The "this report led to a correction" mark, for those who fix problems.
- The anonymous count of listens and openings.
- A section in My Space, the certificate, and a view for Reward with the monthly summary and the list for the
  public thanks.
- The consent for public thanks.
